# CLOSING OF THE JOURNEY

Our journey ends here. But Colombia… does not.

What we traveled through today fits within just a few steps. Beyond this place, an immense country is waiting to be discovered. Mountains we may never have climbed. Rivers we have yet to see. Forests we have yet to hear. Stories we still don't know.

Perhaps one day, when you find yourself standing before one of those landscapes, you’ll remember that once, you walked through it here. And we hope this small journey leaves you with something: the desire to discover Colombia. To explore it. To be amazed by it. To love it. And to protect it.

Because sometimes, to protect a place… first, we have to fall in love with it.

And it can all begin with something very small.
