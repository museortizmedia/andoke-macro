# JADE VINE

As you continue downhill, look for the jade vine. When it blooms, its blue-green color is hard to forget. In its native habitat in the Philippines, its flowers are pollinated by bats. Yes—bats. When we think of pollinators, we usually picture bees or butterflies. But nature has many other workers.

And if you find any fallen jade flowers, take a closer look at their shape. Here at Andoke, we discovered that when you place two of them together, they look like the wings of a butterfly. Discover it for yourself by placing a flower on each ear to create your own "Andokian earrings".
