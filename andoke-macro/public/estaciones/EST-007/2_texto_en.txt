# BUTTERFLY HOUSE

You have just discovered one of nature’s most extraordinary transformations. You saw the egg, the caterpillar, the chrysalis… and now you are about to see the result of that transformation.

As you enter, stop for a moment. Look up. The giant leaves, the plants cascading down from above, the light filtering through the greenery, the water, the humidity… And now: look for them. Welcome to Andoke’s butterfly house.

Colombia is a land of butterflies. Around 3,642 species have been recorded in our country—nearly 20% of all known butterfly species on the planet. Think about that for a moment: almost one out of every five. And why are there so many? Because Colombia is home to an extraordinary variety of mountains, rainforests, forests, páramos, savannas, and coastlines. Altitude, temperature, rainfall, and vegetation all change from one place to another… and with every change come new possibilities for life. Each butterfly depends on specific plants and environmental conditions to complete its life cycle.

Now, begin making your way slowly upward. There’s no need to rush. As you go, notice the butterflies that appear along the way. Some will catch your attention immediately. Others, you’ll have to discover among the leaves and shadows. You don’t need to know their names. Just watch them.

Continue upward until you find three large butterflies gathered together, almost as if they were having a conversation. When you reach them… sit with them. This place deserves a pause.
