# THE SOUTHERN ANDES AND THE MASSIF

Before you enter, look at the large stone in front of you. This spiral was carved by hand by Alejandro using a chisel. It is the Cosmic Churo. A form that speaks to us of cycles, movement, and connection. Perhaps there is no better gateway to begin this journey.

Now… step into Colombia. The land begins to rise. You are entering through the southern Colombian Andes: a land of volcanoes, mountains, and lagoons. Before you are Chiles, Cumbal, and Azufral. Nearby are La Cocha Lagoon and the Galeras Volcano. But don’t try to remember all those names. There is something more important we want you to discover. Look for the water.

Watch it flow downhill. Here, we represent one of the keys to understanding our territory: the Colombian Massif, a vast mountainous region associated with the headwaters of some of the country’s most important rivers. That small stream in front of you represents something immense: the mountain becoming water. And the water that begins in a mountain never stays there. It travels. It connects. It transforms landscapes hundreds of kilometers away.

Now, raise your eyes a little. San Agustín reminds us that this journey is not only about landscapes. Along the way, we will also encounter traces of the cultures that have learned to inhabit, interpret, and give meaning to these territories.

Keep going. Because very soon… the mountains will meet the ocean.
