# THE GATHERING

For thousands of years, different cultures have seen butterflies as more than just animals. In ancient Greece, the butterfly came to represent the human soul. And in different Mesoamerican cultures, butterflies were associated—depending on each tradition—with life, death, regeneration, and transformation. Cultures separated by great distances found something deeply human in them: the possibility of transformation.

Perhaps we can understand why. A caterpillar grows until its skin becomes too tight, and it must shed it. Then comes the chrysalis. From the outside, it may seem as though nothing is happening… but inside, everything is changing. And finally… wings.

Our lives do not work like the metamorphosis of a butterfly. But perhaps we can recognize something of ourselves in that journey. We grow too. We change. Sometimes, we need to leave behind what no longer fits us. And we go through moments when, from the outside, it may seem as though nothing is happening… even though, inside, we are transforming.

Perhaps that is why butterflies continue to fascinate us. Not only because of their colors or their wings, but because they remind us of something essential: life is constantly transforming.

Before you get up, take a moment to think: What moment of transformation are you in right now? You don’t have to answer. Just take the question with you.

When you’re ready, continue making your way upward.
