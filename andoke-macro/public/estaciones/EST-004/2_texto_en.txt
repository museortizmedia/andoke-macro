# THE WATER GARDEN

Continue to the water garden and stop for a moment. At first glance, it may look like just a pond. But take a closer look. There are fish, aquatic plants, insects that come and go, floating leaves, and tiny organisms that often go unnoticed.

What’s fascinating is that none of them exists entirely on its own. The plants provide shelter and help maintain the conditions of the water. Insects find food and places to reproduce. The fish also play a role in this small, delicate balance. And while all of this is happening, other visitors arrive uninvited: dragonflies, birds, and occasionally, frogs.

This garden reminds us of something simple: for life to appear, we first have to create the conditions that allow it to thrive. And that is exactly what has happened at Andoke. Many of the animals that visit these gardens today were not brought here. They found water, food, plants, and shelter… and they came on their own.

Now, continue walking toward the auditorium.
